# Title options

1. **Descriptive:** Oscillatory hydrodynamics of a soft core–shell particle near a fluid–porous interface with stress jump
2. **Method-centred:** Unsteady Stokes–Brinkman reflection theory for a core–shell sphere oscillating near a permeable biological layer
3. **Result/claim-centred:** Porous walls shift and suppress the added-mass crossover of an oscillating soft particle
4. **Problem-centred:** How does a permeable biological interface modify the frequency-dependent drag and added mass of a nearby soft cell or vesicle?
5. **Short "Name: subtitle":** Hearing the glycocalyx: frequency-dependent drag and added mass of a soft particle oscillating near a Brinkman interface

**Recommended: option 1**, possibly with option 3's claim moved into the abstract. It is the clearest for Physics of Fluids, carries the key words (oscillatory, soft core–shell, fluid–porous, stress jump), and continues the title style of your 2021 and 2024 papers. Option 5 is catchier but risks reading as a biology paper.
