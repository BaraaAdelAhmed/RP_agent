\section{Linear system for the core--shell sphere}\label{app:sphere}
For each block of \cref{eq:fields} let $(\mathcal U,\mathcal V,\mathcal S_{rr},\mathcal S_{r\theta})$ be the amplitudes
of $u_r$, $u_\theta$ and the tractions \cref{eq:tractions}. They follow from \cref{eq:vort_radial} with $g=h_j$ or $i_j$,
from the potential blocks, and from the pressures of \cref{lem:decomp}:
\begin{align*}
  \be:&\quad \mathcal U=1,\ \mathcal V=-1,\ \mathcal P=c_j\,r,\\
  \nabla(\be\cdot\nabla r^{-1}):&\quad \mathcal U=2r^{-3},\ \mathcal V=r^{-3},\ \mathcal P=-c_j\,r^{-2},
\end{align*}
with $c_1=i\Wo^2$ in the fluid and $c_2=i\Wo^2-\alpha_s^2$ in the shell. The rigid part $\be$ of \cref{eq:field_shell}
carries the pressure $i\Wo^2\be\cdot\bx$, so its amplitudes are $\mathcal U=1$, $\mathcal V=-1$,
$\mathcal S_{rr}=-i\Wo^2$ and $\mathcal S_{r\theta}=0$ at $r=1$. In terms of these amplitudes the boundary conditions
\cref{eq:bc_core,eq:bc_shell} read:
\begin{align*}
  r=\gamma:&\quad \mathcal U^{(2)\prime}=0,\qquad \mathcal V^{(2)\prime}-\frac{\hat\ell_c}{\mu_s/\mu}\,\mathcal S^{(2)\prime}_{r\theta}=0,\\
  r=1:&\quad \mathcal U^{(1)}=\mathcal U^{(2)},\quad \mathcal V^{(1)}=\mathcal V^{(2)},\quad
  \mathcal S^{(1)}_{rr}=\mathcal S^{(2)}_{rr},\quad
  \mathcal S^{(2)}_{r\theta}-\mathcal S^{(1)}_{r\theta}=\beta_s\alpha_s\left(\mathcal V^{(1)}+1\right),
\end{align*}
where a prime denotes the shell field without its rigid part and $\mathcal S^{(2)}$ uses $\mu_s/\mu$. To avoid
overflow when $|\lambda_s|$ or $|\lambda_f|$ is large, the kernels are rescaled as $h_s(r)e^{\lambda_s\gamma}$,
$i_s(r)e^{-\lambda_s}$, $h_f(r)e^{\lambda_f}$ and $i_f(r)e^{-\lambda_f}$, and the rescaling is undone afterwards.
The solver reproduces \cref{eq:rigid_BC,eq:rigid_Z} to machine precision, and the Debye--Bueche--Brinkman drag of a
homogeneous porous sphere ($\gamma=0$, $\beta_s=0$, $\mu_s=\mu$, $\Wo\to0$),
$\hat F_\infty/(-6\pi)=2\alpha_s^2(1-\tanh\alpha_s/\alpha_s)/[2\alpha_s^2+3(1-\tanh\alpha_s/\alpha_s)]$, to within the
$O(\Wo)$ unsteady correction (\cref{tab:validation}).

\section{Interface system in Fourier space}\label{app:interface}
For a plane mode $\propto e^{i\bk\cdot\bx_\parallel+sz}$ with velocity amplitude $\hat\bu$ and pressure amplitude
$\hat p$, the tractions at $z=0$ in a medium of viscosity $\mu_j$ are
\begin{equation*}
  \hat\sigma_{xz}=\mu_j\left(s\hat u_x+ik_x\hat u_z\right),\quad
  \hat\sigma_{yz}=\mu_j\left(s\hat u_y+ik_y\hat u_z\right),\quad
  \hat\sigma_{zz}=-\hat p+2\mu_js\hat u_z .
\end{equation*}
The mode vectors are: potential, $\hat\bu=(ik_x,ik_y,s)$ with $\hat p=c_j$ and $s=\mp k$; poloidal,
$\hat\bu=(ik_xs,ik_ys,k^2)$ with $\hat p=0$ and $s=-q$ (reflected) or $s=q_p$ (transmitted); toroidal,
$\hat\bu=(ik_y,-ik_x,0)$ with $\hat p=0$. Here $c_1=i\Wo^2$ and $c_3=i\Wo^2-\alpha_p^2$. With superscripts
``inc'', $R$ and $T$, the six rows of \cref{eq:interface_system} are
\begin{align*}
  &\hat\bu^{R}-\hat\bu^{T}=-\hat\bu^{\rm inc}\quad(3\ \text{rows}),\qquad
  \hat\sigma^{R}_{zz}-\hat\sigma^{T}_{zz}=-\hat\sigma^{\rm inc}_{zz},\\
  &\hat\sigma^{T}_{\tau z}-\hat\sigma^{R}_{\tau z}-\beta_p\alpha_p\hat u^{R}_\tau
   =\hat\sigma^{\rm inc}_{\tau z}+\beta_p\alpha_p\hat u^{\rm inc}_\tau\quad(\tau=x,y).
\end{align*}
The columns of $\mathsf A$ are normalised to unit Euclidean norm before solving; this removes the $O(k)$ degeneracy of
the mode vectors as $k\to0$. For a rigid wall the system reduces to $\hat\bu^R=-\hat\bu^{\rm inc}$ ($3\times3$).
